   # Daily Digest – Privacy Policy
   Daily Digest is a personal application used only by its developer.
   It accesses the developer's own Gmail and Google Calendar (read-only) and sends email only to the developer.
   Data is stored in the developer's private database and is never sold or shared with third parties.
   Redacted text may be processed by an AI API solely to generate the developer's own summary.
